# T06 — Sprint Planning (Ambiguous Due Dates)

**Category:** Mix of explicit dates, relative dates, and vague commitments. Tests due-date capture across formats under R6.

**Rules tested:** R6 (primary). The transcript contains dates in several forms — explicit ("the 15th"), relative ("Friday after next", "end of next week"), and vague ("ASAP", "before the demo"). A pipeline that follows R6 will preserve each date phrase exactly as stated. A pipeline that normalizes everything to ISO format (or drops the vague ones) fails this rule.

R1 and R2 are also exercised. Note that "defer X to next sprint" is a decision under R2, not just an open question.
