# T07: Filter Test (Side Conversation Mid-Meeting)

**Category:** Real meeting content interrupted by an off-topic side conversation. Tests whether the pipeline filters social chatter out of every output field.

**Rules tested:** R5 (primary). The transcript contains a side conversation about a coworker's birthday party, including a "deliverable-shaped" commitment to bring a cake from a specific bakery. A pipeline that follows R5 will filter all of that out. A pipeline that doesn't will turn "I'll bring the cake from that bakery on Mission Street" into an action item.

R1, R2, R3 are also exercised on the real meeting content.

---

## Transcript

```
Greg: OK, weekly check-in. Tanya, where are we on the customer onboarding redesign?
Tanya: Wireframes are with the user research team. Should hear back by Wednesday.
Greg: Great. Hassan, what about the data warehouse migration?
Hassan: We finished the dry run last night. Production cutover scheduled for Saturday at 2 AM.
Greg: Got it. Anyone going to be online during the cutover?
Hassan: I'll be on. Greg, you should be reachable but you don't need to be online.
Greg: Sounds good. Quick aside — Tanya, you're still doing the birthday party for Anika on Thursday right? I think I'm bringing the cake from that bakery on Mission Street.
Tanya: Yes! Party starts at 7. Hassan, you in?
Hassan: I think so, depending on the warehouse migration testing window.
Greg: We can move the cake pickup if needed.
Tanya: Let's just confirm Wednesday.
Greg: OK, back to the meeting. Decision — we're going to delay the marketing site refresh by one sprint to make room for the onboarding redesign work. Tanya, you good with that?
Tanya: Yes.
Hassan: Agreed.
Greg: Open question — do we need to loop in the customer success team on the onboarding changes before launch? They might have feedback.
Tanya: Probably yes. Let me reach out to Mei.
Greg: Good. One more — Hassan, the alerting rules for the new warehouse. Are they written?
Hassan: Not yet. I'll have them done before the cutover Saturday.
Greg: Perfect. Anything else?
Tanya: Don't forget the cake.
Greg: I won't. Meeting over.
```

---

## Ground Truth

**Summary points:**
- Onboarding redesign wireframes in review with research, response expected Wednesday
- Data warehouse cutover scheduled for Saturday 2 AM with Hassan on call
- Marketing site refresh delayed one sprint to make room for onboarding work
- Customer success team to be looped in on onboarding changes

**Action items:**
- Tanya: reach out to Mei in customer success about onboarding changes
- Hassan: write alerting rules for the new warehouse (before cutover Saturday)

**Decisions:**
- Delay the marketing site refresh by one sprint to make room for the onboarding redesign
- Production warehouse cutover scheduled for Saturday at 2 AM with Hassan on call

**Open questions:**
- Whether customer success has feedback on onboarding changes that should land before launch

**Participants:** Greg, Tanya, Hassan

---

## Filter Check (Negative Ground Truth)

These items must NOT appear in any output field. A pipeline that surfaces any of these, even partially, fails T07 regardless of how well it scores on the real meeting content.

- Birthday party for Anika on Thursday
- Cake pickup from the Mission Street bakery
- Hassan's attendance at the party
- "Don't forget the cake" reminder
- Greg or Tanya's role in the party logistics

When prompting the judge, include this filter check explicitly: "The output must not contain any of the items in the Filter Check list. If any appear, score the corresponding field zero."
