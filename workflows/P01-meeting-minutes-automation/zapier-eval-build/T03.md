# T03: Discovery Meeting (No Decisions)

**Category:** Pure exploration. Multiple action items and open questions, zero decisions. Tests whether the pipeline avoids hallucinating decisions when none were made.

**Rules tested:** R2 (primary). The transcript explicitly opens with "we're not deciding anything today." A pipeline that follows R2 will produce an empty decisions list. A pipeline that doesn't will turn provisional discussion into false decisions.

---

## Transcript

```
Rachel: Thanks everyone. This is a scoping conversation for the Stripe integration. We're not deciding anything today — just figuring out what we don't know.
Tom: Sounds good. First question — are we doing this with Stripe Connect or just basic Stripe Payments?
Rachel: That's exactly what I want to figure out. Tom, can you spec out what each option would mean for our marketplace use case?
Tom: I can do that this week.
Rachel: Devi, what about compliance? We're going to be moving money for sellers. Does that change our regulatory footprint?
Devi: Maybe. I need to talk to our compliance counsel before I can answer. I'll set that up.
Rachel: OK. What else are we missing?
Tom: We should probably understand what our current payment volumes look like. The Stripe pricing changes a lot at different tiers.
Rachel: Good point. I can pull the numbers from the finance dashboard.
Devi: One more — what's the migration story for our existing payment integrations? Are we ripping out the old PayPal flow entirely?
Rachel: Open question. I don't know yet.
Tom: I'd want to know that before scoping anything.
Rachel: Fair. Let me put together a doc with everything we don't know and we can reconvene next week.
Tom: Sounds good.
Devi: Works for me.
```
