# T02 — Long Messy Quarterly Planning

**Category:** Long meeting, multiple topics, some ambiguity in ownership. Tests whether the pipeline can extract structure from a real planning session without missing items or inventing owners.

**Rules tested:** R1, R2, R3, R4, R6. Volume and topic-switching test the pipeline's ability to apply R1/R2/R3 consistently across a longer transcript. R6 exercises date capture across multiple formats. R3 includes a blocker (logo placement) that prevents downstream work.

---

## Transcript

```
David: Alright, Q3 marketing planning. Big agenda. Let's start with the campaign calendar. Priya, you've been working on this.
Priya: Yeah. I've got three campaigns slotted for Q3 — the back-to-school push in August, the partner co-marketing thing with Acme in September, and the brand refresh announcement at the end of the quarter.
David: The Acme one — are we locked on that?
Priya: We're verbally locked. Their legal still hasn't returned the co-marketing agreement. I'll chase them this week.
Carlos: While we're on Acme, did we ever resolve the logo placement thing?
Priya: No, that's still open.
David: Note that as a blocker. We can't announce until that's resolved. Carlos, what's the website refresh look like?
Carlos: We're going to launch the new homepage on August 5th. The pricing page will follow two weeks later. Blog template is last, no firm date.
David: Let's lock August 5th for the homepage. That's a yes. Carlos, you own that.
Carlos: Got it.
David: Pricing page on the 19th then. Same owner.
Carlos: Yes.
David: Maya, how's the demand gen pipeline looking?
Maya: We're tracking against the quota but the new SDR ramp is slow. I might need to hire one more if we want to hit Q4 numbers.
David: Open question — are we OK funding another SDR slot? I need to take that to finance. Maya, send me your justification doc by next Tuesday.
Maya: Will do.
David: One last thing. Conference plans. We've got three on the calendar — SaaStr, INBOUND, and Dreamforce. Do we keep all three or cut one?
Priya: I think we cut Dreamforce. Bad ROI last year.
Carlos: Agree.
Maya: I'm fine cutting it.
David: OK, decision — we're cutting Dreamforce from Q3. Priya, please cancel the booth contract.
Priya: I'll get on it.
David: Great. Let's wrap.
```

---

## Ground Truth

**Summary points:**
- Three Q3 campaigns planned (back-to-school, Acme co-marketing, brand refresh)
- Website refresh anchored to August 5th homepage launch, August 19th pricing page
- Demand gen tracking but SDR ramp slow
- Dreamforce cut from Q3 conference plans

**Action items:**
- Priya: chase Acme legal on co-marketing agreement (this week)
- Carlos: launch new homepage (August 5th)
- Carlos: launch new pricing page (August 19th)
- Maya: send David the SDR hire justification doc (by next Tuesday)
- Priya: cancel the Dreamforce booth contract

**Decisions:**
- Lock August 5th for homepage launch
- Cut Dreamforce from Q3 conference plans

**Open questions:**
- Acme logo placement is still unresolved and blocks the announcement
- Whether to fund another SDR slot (pending finance review)
- Firm date for the blog template launch

**Participants:** David, Priya, Carlos, Maya
