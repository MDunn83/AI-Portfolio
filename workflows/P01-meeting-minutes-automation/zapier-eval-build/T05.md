# T05 — International Sync (Weird Names)

**Category:** Names that LLMs commonly mangle, anglicize, or simplify. Tests participant extraction and owner-on-action-item assignment under R4.

**Rules tested:** R4 (primary). A pipeline that follows R4 will preserve "Søren", "Niamh", "Lakshmi", "Kazumi", and "Aaliyah" exactly as written, both in the participants list and in action item owners. A pipeline that drops the diacritic on Søren, normalizes Niamh to "Niav", or shortens Lakshmi to "Laks" fails this rule.

R1, R2, R3 are also exercised at baseline. The transcript also contains a deferred topic ("table it for the architecture review"), which is a decision under R2, not an open question.
